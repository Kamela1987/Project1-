# Client Templates

Reusable Word templates for Kenstar General Dealers' CV and cover letter service. Duplicate these per client rather than editing the originals.

## `ATS_CV_Template.docx`

- Single-column layout — no tables, text boxes, columns, or headers/footers, so Applicant Tracking Systems can parse it cleanly.
- Standard fonts (Calibri) and simple bold/color section headings — no graphics or icons that ATS software can't read.
- Sections: Professional Summary, Key Skills, Professional Experience (3 role slots), Education, Certifications, References.
- Every `[bracketed placeholder]` is meant to be replaced with the client's real details.

**Per-client workflow:**
1. Copy the file and rename it, e.g. `CV_JaneMwansa.docx`.
2. Replace the name/contact line.
3. Rewrite the Professional Summary and Key Skills to mirror keywords from the specific job description the client is applying to — this is what makes it "job-aligned," not just ATS-formatted.
4. Fill in real employment history with achievement-focused, quantified bullet points (numbers, %, amounts).
5. Delete any unused job/education slots; duplicate a job block if the client has more than three roles.

## `Cover_Letter_Template.docx`

- Matches the CV's header/contact style for a consistent client package.
- Five-paragraph structure: opening (role + source), body 1 (relevant achievement), body 2 (soft skills + why this company), closing (call to action), sign-off.
- Every `[bracketed placeholder]` must be personalized per job application — cover letters should never be reused verbatim across employers.

**Per-client workflow:**
1. Copy and rename, e.g. `CoverLetter_JaneMwansa_CompanyX.docx`.
2. Fill in the hiring manager/company details.
3. Personalize each paragraph to the specific job posting and company — especially the "why this company" line.

## `Interview_Prep_Notes_Template.docx`

For clients after they've been called for an interview — delivered as a standalone briefing document, not tied to the CV/cover letter package.

- Sections: Snapshot (logistics), Company Snapshot, Role Requirements Recap, Your Talking Points, Likely Questions & Answer Notes (Introductory, Behavioral/STAR, Role-Specific, Situational), Questions to Ask the Interviewer, Logistics Checklist, Day-Of Reminders.
- Answer notes are structured around the **STAR method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral questions.

**Per-client workflow:**
1. Copy and rename, e.g. `InterviewPrep_JaneMwansa_CompanyX.docx`.
2. Fill in interview logistics (date, time, format, interviewer names if known).
3. Research the company (mission, recent news, culture) — this is what makes the prep feel tailored rather than generic.
4. Pull the client's strongest achievements from their CV into "Your Talking Points," matched to the job requirements.
5. Replace the role-specific/technical placeholder questions with ones likely for that actual role (e.g. a sales role gets pipeline/quota questions; a dev role gets technical/system-design questions).
6. Fill in real "Notes" under each question with talking points drawn from the client's actual experience — not generic advice.

## `Business_Plan_Template.docx`

A full business plan for funding applications, loan requests, or PACRA registration support.

- Cover page, then 9 numbered sections: Executive Summary, Company Description, Market Analysis, Organization & Management, Products & Services, Marketing & Sales Strategy, Funding Request, Financial Projections, Appendix.
- Written to stand on its own for a bank, investor, or grant reviewer who has never met the client.

**Per-client workflow:**
1. Copy and rename, e.g. `BusinessPlan_KenstarClientX.docx`.
2. Fill in the Company Description and Market Analysis first — everything else follows from those.
3. Write the Executive Summary **last**, even though it appears first — it should summarize the finished plan, not be written blind.
4. If the client has detailed financials prepared separately (e.g. in Excel), reference them in Financial Projections and attach as an appendix rather than retyping tables here.
5. Drop the Funding Request section entirely if the plan is for internal use only, not external funding.

## `Company_Profile_Template.docx`

A shorter, brochure-style one-pager for tenders, partner introductions, or general business marketing — distinct from the full Business Plan.

- Sections: Company Overview, Company Details (registration/contact info), Mission & Vision, Core Services/Products, Why Choose Us, Leadership Team, Clients & Achievements, Contact Us.
- Designed to be skimmed in under two minutes by a tender evaluator or prospective partner.

**Per-client workflow:**
1. Copy and rename, e.g. `CompanyProfile_ClientBusinessName.docx`.
2. Fill in Company Details first (registration number, contact info) — this is what tenders check first.
3. Keep "Why Choose Us" to 3 sharp, specific differentiators — avoid generic claims like "quality service" with nothing to back it up.
4. If the client has a logo, add it to the top of the cover section (keep it simple — a single centered image, no text boxes).

## Notes

- All five files were generated programmatically and validated against the OOXML schema, but always open and proofread in Word/LibreOffice before sending to a client.
- Keep placeholder brackets `[ ]` intact only in the master templates — every client copy should have them fully replaced.
